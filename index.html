About Tawer Academy

A place where English stops being a subject and becomes your voice.

Tawer means growth, and that's what we're about. We started with one classroom and a simple idea: people learn a language by using it, not by memorising rules. Today our teachers guide thousands of students through every level, from complete beginners to professionals preparing for IELTS or a job abroad.

To be the most trusted English academy in the region, where every learner leaves able to study, work and live in English.

How do I know which level to join?

Take our free 3-minute online placement test, then a short speaking check with a teacher during your free trial lesson. We place you in the right CEFR level, from A1 to C1.

How many students are in a class?

Group classes have a maximum of 8 students, so everyone speaks in every lesson. Kids' classes are capped at 6.

What ages do you teach?

We teach children from 6 years old, teenagers and adults. Kids and teens are grouped by age and level, with playful, project-based lessons.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes. After passing each level's final assessment you receive a Tawer Academy certificate stating your CEFR level, which you can add to your CV or university application.

Can I study online?

Absolutely. Every course runs in-class and live online with the same teachers, materials and certificate. You can switch between the two at any time.

What if I miss a lesson?

All online lessons are recorded, and you can join a make-up class in another group at the same level during the same week.
