    About Tawer Academy

    Built by teachers who wanted English to feel alive.

    Tawer means growth. We help learners of every age turn English from a school subject into a skill they actually use, in class, at work and abroad.

    Our story, since 2017

    It started with two teachers, one rented classroom and six students who were tired of memorising grammar tables. We built every lesson around real conversation, and word spread. Today Tawer teaches learners from age 6 to 60, in class and live online.
